# Entity lifecycle and pools

This area spans the fixed-size entity pool, type-specific construction and teardown, deferred object-list creation, and several adjacent state/resource helpers. Statements below describe the retained pseudocode; raw bits and globals are left unnamed where their consumers do not establish a reliable domain meaning. Existing findings 05, 248, 315, 326, 360, and 393 cover several of these helpers; this finding joins them through lifecycle and caller flow.

## Entity record and lifecycle flow

- The pool is a chunk table at `DAT_004de790`, with a free-list head at `DAT_004de810`. Chunk capacities grow as powers of two; each record is `0x40c` bytes and uses `+0x408` as the free-list link or the live-record self-pointer checked by the walker. These are observed structural invariants, not semantic field labels.
- Creation flows `EntityCreateWithPosition` → `EntityCreateByType` → `PooledRecordAllocateAndReset` (and possibly chunk growth), then initializes a kind-specific component, position, common activity state, and count tables. Deferred object-list creation queues records first and later enters the same constructor path.
- Teardown reverses references before recycling the record: removes linked command/activity/registry state, invokes cleanup callbacks, releases the component selected by entity kind, then links the record onto the free list. This explains why consumers distinguish live records by `entity+0x408 == entity` and why the pool walker’s optional exclusion bit matters.
- Grid count arrays index `(kind, subtype, slot)` into `0x12c0` dwords; the observed flattening formula is `(subtype + kind * 0xf) * 0x10 + slot`. Their exact gameplay quantity remains uncertain despite their creation/destruction updates.
