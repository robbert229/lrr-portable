## Path relationships

`UpdateRenderViewportState @ 0x426250` refreshes viewport state through `0x45d8a0` and then invokes `TerrainViewTileGeometryBuild`. The builder scans grid coordinates, asks `TerrainTileStyleResolve` whether a tile should contribute style data, transforms tile geometry through `ViewPointToScreenGeometryTransform`, and emits vertices in groups of four until its 200-vertex scratch batch is full. It submits the batch and later sends the viewport bounds and tile data through additional helpers. This links terrain visibility, viewport projection, and geometry submission; the meanings of the style cases and renderer command payloads remain partly opaque.

The input path in `TerrainPointerCommandUpdate @ 0x425d90` applies the rectangle test, calls `TerrainPointPickResolve` to map screen coordinates into the terrain grid, and uses `ViewportPointCallbackDispatch` for a separate callback route. The same point mapper also participates directly in selection and pointer behavior described in Finding 253. The half-open boundary convention means points on the right or bottom edge are outside the rectangle.

`UiRenderRecordCreate`/`UiRenderRecordFree` and `EntityTypeAndStateFlagPredicate` are assigned to this area, but their direct consumers are not established by the checked-in pseudocode or cited caller records. `TexturedSceneObjectDrawAtPosition`'s direct body likewise only establishes the transformed circle drawing sequence, so its current name may encode context outside the direct body.

## Focused follow-up slices

- Resolve the consumer and record layout for `0x45da90`/`0x45daf0`, and establish whether they feed the viewport rendering path.
- Trace any indirect or dynamically registered caller of `0x45e400`; identify the type and flag domains before assigning semantic names.
- Identify the context behind the `TexturedSceneObjectDrawAtPosition` label and verify the role of its circle-drawing arguments.
- Resolve the terrain style case values and renderer descriptor consumed by `0x45dbc0`.
