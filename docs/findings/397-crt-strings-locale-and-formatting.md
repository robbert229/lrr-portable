# Finding 397 — CRT Strings, Locale, and Formatting

This finding maps the 61 functions assigned to `platform-and-runtime/runtime-strings-locale-and-format`. Evidence is the canonical decompile corpus at `docs/decompiles/full/LEGORR.full.c`. Caller/callee claims refer to direct calls in that corpus unless marked as an inference. The area contains CRT compatibility routines and game-facing string/config helpers; several aliases and ABI details remain unresolved, so descriptions follow the recovered operations rather than assuming modern CRT contracts.

## Error and state boundaries

- Code-page, LCID, ctype, timezone, argv, environment, signal, and locale-conversion state is cached in globals. The helpers update related parts of those globals in separate paths; the corpus does not prove thread safety or reentrancy for these lazy initializers.
- Several functions return CRT/Win32-style sentinels (`0`, `-1`, comparison constants) and set a shared error global on conversion/allocation failure. Callers differ in whether they propagate the sentinel, terminate through `__amsg_exit`, or continue with fallback behavior.
- `CrtCompareStringACompat`, `CrtGetStringTypeACompat`, and locale case conversion have platform capability probes. Their ANSI and wide fallbacks do not all share the same parameter conventions; only the direct argument flow is asserted here.
- `ParseSignedDecimal` and integer formatting routines do not show bounds/radix validation in their bodies. This finding records those limits; it does not establish safety for arbitrary external inputs.
