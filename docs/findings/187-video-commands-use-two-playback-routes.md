# Finding 187 — Configured Videos Use DirectShow; Credits Use AVIFIL32

The credits background path and the boot/level video paths do not share one playback implementation. `FUN_0040a0c0`, reached from the Credits callback, opens its movie with `AVIStreamOpenFromFileA`, queries `AVIStreamInfoA`, and fetches frames with `AVIStreamGetFrame`. In contrast, configured boot and level videos go through `FUN_00472560` and `FUN_00472390`; that route calls `CoInitialize`, creates the DirectShow `AMMultiMediaStream` object with `CoCreateInstance`, and invokes its `IAMMultiMediaStream` methods through a vtable. It adds primary video and primary audio streams before opening the file (finding 189). The recovered class/interface GUIDs and call sequence match Microsoft's documented multimedia-stream creation path. The executable imports both the AVIFIL32 stream APIs and the COM creation APIs.

The separate call chains explain why the AVIFIL32 stream-selection result in finding 177 does not describe configured boot/level video playback.

**Evidence.** Shipped `reference/LegoRR.exe`, SHA-256 `c3d8ce23f1aa21814950c77e713ec176599506d4e71758cf1cf709525e581e0f`. `FUN_00415910` and `FUN_00415990` load configured boot/level video files through `FUN_00472560`; `FUN_00472390` at `0x472390` calls `CoInitialize`, and `FUN_004722b0` at `0x4722b0` calls `CoCreateInstance` with the `AMMultiMediaStream` class and `IAMMultiMediaStream` interface GUIDs. Its vtable calls at `+0x30`, `+0x3c`, and `+0x40` align with the documented `Initialize`, `AddMediaStream`, and `OpenFile` sequence. Microsoft's [multimedia streaming object](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/directshow/multimedia-streaming-object-and-interface-hierarchy) reference identifies the object/interface and its DirectShow role; the [video streaming sample](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/directshow/video-streaming-sample-code) uses the same CoCreateInstance route. The Credits route reaches `FUN_0040a0c0` at `0x40a0c0` via `FUN_004103d0`, and its `FUN_0047e800`/`FUN_0047eb20` path calls the AVIFIL32 stream functions. `objdump -p reference/LegoRR.exe` lists `CoInitialize`, `CoCreateInstance`, and the AVI stream imports. The functions are in `docs/decompiles/full/LEGORR.full.c`.
